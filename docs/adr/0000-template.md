# NNNN. Short title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Deprecated
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: maintainer
- Plan reference: section or decision number in `docs/design/implementation-plan.md`, if any

## Context

What forces are at play? State the problem, the constraints (technical, security,
legal, schedule) and the facts the decision rests on. Cite the plan section or a dated
source for anything that is not obvious. Keep it short enough that a new contributor
can read it in two minutes.

## Decision

What we will do, in plain sentences. Name the concrete choice and its boundaries (what
is in, what is explicitly out). If a spike or test will confirm the decision, name it
here and say what result would change it.

## Alternatives considered

Real options we weighed, each with an honest trade-off, including what made it
attractive. One short paragraph or bullet per option. Do not list options nobody would
choose.

## Consequences

### Good

- What gets easier or safer.

### Bad

- What gets harder, slower or riskier, and what we accept by choosing this.

### Follow-ups

- Work, tests or documents this decision creates. Link them when they exist.
